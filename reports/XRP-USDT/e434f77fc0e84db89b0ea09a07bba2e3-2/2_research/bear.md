### 1. Moving Averages aur Stagnation ka Fareeb
Aap keh rahe hain ke price $1.49 par hai aur 50 SMA ($1.40) ya 200 SMA ($1.28) se oopar hone ki wajah se trend bullish hai. Bhai, yeh "bullishness" nahi balkay **stagnation (thakawat)** hai!

- Long-term moving averages se oopar hona is baat ki guarantee nahi hai ke price hamesha ooper hi jayegi. Jab market mein koi direct catalyst ya news flow hi nahi hai (jaisa ke report saaf bata rahi hai ke pichle ek hafte mein XRP-USDT ke liye koi news nahi aayi), toh yeh moving averages sirf ek kamzor sahara ban kar reh jati hain. Agar zara sa bhi selling pressure aaya, toh yeh saaray levels tinka ban kar bikhar jayenge.

Jab market mein buyers ki aamarat hi khatam ho jaye, toh price Upper Band ($1.64) ko choone ke bajaye seedha Lower Band ($1.30) ki taraf girti hai.
