### 1. Moving Averages ka Dhoka aur "Consolidation" ka Asal Sach
Aapne kaha ke price ($1.50) apne 50-day SMA ($1.41) aur 200-day SMA ($1.28) se oopar hai, isliye yeh ek strong uptrend hai. Bhai, moving averages peechhay chalne wale indicators (lagging indicators) hote hain! Jab tak yeh oopar dikhate hain, tab tak momentum khatam ho chuka hota hai. 
* Agar aap MACD readings ko dekhen (`macd: 0.03`, `macds: 0.04`, `macdh: -0.01`), toh histogram saaf manfi (negative) taraf ja raha hai. Iska matlab hai ke short-term momentum mar chuka hai. 
* Yeh sirf "breather" nahi hai, yeh **exhaustion** hai. Jab price $1.50 par 10-day EMA ke sath chipki hui ho aur koi naya buyer market mein enter na ho raha ho, toh isay accumulation nahi balkay **distribution** ya stagnation kehte hain. Agar support $1.31 (Lower Bollinger Band) ki taraf tooti, toh saare bulls dekhte reh jayenge!

Yeh retail ka "soye rehna" nahi hai, yeh is baat ka saboot hai ke market ko XRP mein koi khaas jaan nazar nahi aa rahi. Asal catalysts ka mukammal sikaqaad hai—pichle haftay asset-specific aik bhi positive news ya press release samne nahi aayi.
