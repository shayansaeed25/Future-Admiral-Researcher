Iska matlab yeh hota hai ke asset apne mean (average) se bohot door ja chuka hai aur gravity apna kaam zaroor karegi. Jab correction aayegi na, toh yeh 1.28 (200 SMA) ki taraf wapas bhagega aur yeh saara gain pal bhar mein saaf ho jayega.

Agar macro liquidity mein zara sa bhi jhatka laga ya interest rates ke hawale se koi nayi hawai khabar aa gayi, toh sab se pehle yeh speculative aur overextended digital assets hi crash honge.
