Yeh darr nahi, balkay hamari asal zimmedari hai—hamara maqsad firm ke sarmaye (assets) ki hifazat karna aur be-ja volatility se bachna hai. 

Zara market ke h haqaiq (facts) ko ghor se dekhein! `XRP-USDT` ka price is waqt **1.51** par hai aur yeh Bollinger Bands ke middle band (**1.49**) ke bilkul qareeb ek tang range mein phasa hua hai. Iska saaf matlab yeh hai ke momentum thanda par raha hai aur market mein koi wazeh direction nahi hai. Aisi ghair-yaqeeni surat-e-hal mein naye aggressive positions kholna, seedhe taur par firm ke paison ko un-necessary risk mein dhakelna hai.

Aap ne global macro environment aur US stock markets ki record rallies ka hawala diya, lekin yaad rakhein ke crypto markets aur particularly `XRP-USDT` ke liye pichle ek haftay mein koi direct positive news ya fundamental catalyst maujood nahi hai. Global liquidity ya tech stocks ki tezi ka yeh matlab hargiz nahi hai ke yeh asset bhi bina kisi waja ke uper bhagay ga. Market report khud yeh wazeh karti hai ke bina direct news ke, humein strict risk management aur technical levels par bharosa karna chahiye.

Humain short-term ke jazbaati utaar-chadhaw aur FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) mein aakar apnay sarmaye ko daao par nahi lagana chahiye. Stability aur steady growth hamari pehli tarjeeh honi chahiyen, is liye trader ka "Hold" ka faisla hi is waqt sab se behtar aur mehfooz intikhab hai.
