Market ki mojuda condition aur trading strategy ke liye darj zail 8 indicators ka intekhab kiya gaya hai jo baghair kisi redundancy ke mukamal aur diverse insights faraham karte hain:

1. **close_50_sma (50 SMA):** Medium-term trend direction identify karne aur dynamic support/resistance levels ke tor par istemal hota hai.

5. **macd (MACD):** EMAs ke difference ke zariye price momentum aur trend changes ko compute karta hai.
6. **boll (Bollinger Middle):** 20 SMA par mabni dynamic benchmark hai jo price movement aur bands ki wusat ko measure karta hai.
7. **atr (ATR):** Market ki current volatility ko measure karta hai taake risk management aur stop-loss levels theek se set kiye ja saken.
8. **vwma (VWMA):** Volume-weighted moving average hai jo price action ko volume data ke sath integrate kar ke trend ki tasdeeq karta hai.
