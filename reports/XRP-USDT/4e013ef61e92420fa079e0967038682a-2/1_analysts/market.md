### 1. Muntakhib Shuda Indicators aur Inka Maqsad (Selection of Indicators)

Market ki mojooda halat ko samajhnay ke liye hum ne 8 ghair-mutazaad (non-redundant) indicators ka intekhab kiya hai jo mukhtalif categories se taaluq rakhtay hain:
1. **close_10_ema (10 EMA):** Short-term momentum aur qeemat ki fori tabdeelion ko pakarney ke liye.
2. **close_50_sma (50 SMA):** Medium-term trend ki simt aur dynamic support/resistance tay karne ke liye.
3. **close_200_sma (200 SMA):** Long-term market benchmark aur buniyadi trend ki tasdeeq ke liye.
4. **macd (MACD Line):** EMA differences ke zariye momentum aur trend ki tabdeeli maloom karne ke liye.
5. **rsi (Relative Strength Index):** Overbought ya oversold halaat ka pata lagane ke liye (70/30 thresholds).
6. **boll (Bollinger Middle / 20 SMA):** Price movement ka central dynamic benchmark.
7. **atr (Average True Range):** Volatility ki pemaish kar ke stop-loss aur risk management set karne ke liye.
8. **vwma (Volume Weighted Moving Average):** Price action ke sath volume ko integrate kar ke trend ki tasdeeq karne ke liye.
