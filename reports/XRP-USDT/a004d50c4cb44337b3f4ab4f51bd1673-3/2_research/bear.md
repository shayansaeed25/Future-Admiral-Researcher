Market na toh poori tarah oversold hai ke wahan se ek dam se bounce aaye, aur na hi overbought hai ke koi bada breakout ho sake. Yeh ek aisi jagah hai jahan retail investors ko phansaya jata hai taake bade players (whales) apni positions unhein bech kar nikal sakein.
