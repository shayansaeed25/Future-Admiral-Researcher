TAO-USD ke liye market ka detailed aur nuanced technical tajzia (analysis) darj zail hai. Is tajziye mein hum ne muntaqib (selected) kardah 8 behtareen indicators ka istemal kiya hai taake market ki simt (trend), momentum, volatility aur volume ko behtar taur par samjha ja sake.

### Intikhab-e-Indicators (Selected Indicators & Rationale)

1. **close_50_sma (50-Day Simple Moving Average):**
   * *Wajah:* Yeh medium-term trend ki direction ko wazeh karta hai aur dynamic support ya resistance ke taur par kaam karta hai.
2. **close_200_sma (200-Day Simple Moving Average):**
   * *Wajah:* Long-term trend ka benchmark hai jo overall market structure (bullish/bearish) ki tasdeeq karta hai.
3. **close_10_ema (10-Day Exponential Moving Average):**
   * *Wajah:* Short-term momentum aur qareeb-tareen entry/exit points ko pakadne ke liye ek sensitive indicator hai.
4. **macd (Moving Average Convergence Divergence):**
   * *Wajah:* Trend ki tabdili aur momentum mein hone wale crossovers ko detect karne ke liye istemal hota hai.
5. **rsi (Relative Strength Index):**
   * *Wajah:* Overbought aur oversold halaat ki nishandehi karta hai (70/30 thresholds ke zariye) bina kisi redundancy ke.
6. **boll (Bollinger Middle - 20 SMA):**
   * *Wajah:* Price movement ka dynamic benchmark faraham karta hai jo upper aur lower bands ke sath mil kar breakout zones batata hai.
7. **atr (Average True Range):**
   * *Wajah:* Market ki mojooda volatility ki pemaish karta hai taake stop-loss levels ko theek se set kiya ja sake.
8. **vwma (Volume Weighted Moving Average):**
   * *Wajah:* Price action ke sath volume ko integrate karke trend ki authenticity ki tasdeeq karta hai.
