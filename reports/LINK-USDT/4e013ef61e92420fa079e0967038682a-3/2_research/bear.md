### 1. "Accumulation Phase" ya Phir Distribution aur Stalling?
Aapne kaha ke price $14.12 par hai aur 200 SMA (9.55) ya 50 SMA (12.10) se oopar hone ki wajah se yeh accumulation hai. Bhai, zara **MACD histogram ko dekhein jo bilkul 0.00 par khada hai** aur MACD lines neutral hain! Yeh "healthy pause" nahi, balkay **exhaustion (thakawat)** ki nishani hai. Jab price 28 September ko $15.77 ke high par gayi thi aur wahan se sustain nahi kar saki, iska matlab hai ke oopri satah par sellers heavy hain aur woh har relief rally ko dump kar rahe hain. Smart money accumulate nahi kar raha, balkay yahan "distribution" ya profit-booking chal rahi hai.

### 2. RSI aur Bollinger Bands ka Asli Matlab
Aap RSI 61.10 ko "sweet spot" keh kar naye highs ki umeed laga rahe hain, lekin zara yeh bhi sochein ke jab momentum ban hi nahi raha (MACD flat, 10 EMA 13.94 par current price ke bilkul qareeb), toh yeh 61 ka RSI dheere-dheere drift ho kar neeche ki taraf bhi gir sakta hai. Price Bollinger Bands ke middle (13.18) aur upper band (15.60) ke beech phansi hui hai. Agar yeh sachmuch breakout ki tayari hoti, toh volume aur short-term momentum (10 EMA) price ko upper band ki taraf aggressively push kar rahe hote, na ke ek narrow range mein latkaye rakhte.
