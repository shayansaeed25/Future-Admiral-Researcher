Toh phir rasta kya hai? Neutral risk analyst ke taur par mera yeh manna hai ke humein na toh bina soche-samjhe poori tarah aggressive ho kar naye trades kholne chahiye, aur na hi bilkul dar kar baithna chahiye. Behtareen approach yeh hai ke hum apna mojooda exposure hold karein, jaisa ke plan mein hai, aur market ko thoda sa saans lene dein. Agar price thoda sa bhi pullback karti hai aur 12.20 se 12.80 USDT ke zone mein aati hai, tab hum wahan par calculated aur controlled tareeqay se accumulation kar sakte hain. Yahi ek balanced aur sustainable strategy hai jo dono doston ke darr aur josh ke darmiyan behtareen tawazun qaim karti hai.
