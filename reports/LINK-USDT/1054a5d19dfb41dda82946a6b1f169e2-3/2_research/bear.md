Aapne **50 SMA ($12.10)** aur **200 SMA ($9.55)** ka bara rona roya ke price in se oopar hai. Bhai, yaad rakhein ke moving averages hamesha **lagging indicators** hote hain—yeh pichli history batate hain, yeh kal ki guarantee nahi dete! Agar price current level ($14.10) se gir kar Bollinger Middle ($13.18) ya lower band ($10.76) ki taraf aana shuru hui, toh yeh saare moving averages dekhte ke dekhte reh jayenge aur aapka "long-term uptrend" palak jhapakte mein toot jayega. 
Aur agar qeemat wahan tak girti hai, toh current price se neeche ka faasla kitna bara hai? Aik mamooli sa bhi shock is $14.10 ki diwaar ko girane ke liye kaafi hai.
