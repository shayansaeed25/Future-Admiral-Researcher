ATR (`0.80`) moderate volatility dikha raha hai, aur agar hum is waqt bina kisi solid catalyst ke naye aggressive bets le lenge, toh hum khud ko unnecessary downside risk ke samne khara kar denge.

Isliye, hamari primary priority assets ki hifazat aur capital preservation honi chahiye. Jab tak market mein mazeed clear confirmation nahi milti, tab tak conservative approach hi sab se mehfooz aur danishmandana rasta hai.
