
Conservative Analyst: Bhai, Aggressive Analyst sahab ka yeh josh aur blind optimism sun kar lagta hai ke woh risk management ka matlab hi bhool gaye hain. Market mein "bold steps" uthane ke chakkar mein aisi ghaltiyan ho jati hain jo poori capital ko khatre mein daal deti hain. Unka kehna hai ke RSI 67.00 par hai aur abhi 70 se neeche hai isliye foran jump maar dein, lekin woh yeh kyun bhool rahe hain ke price bilkul Upper Bollinger Band (14.51) ke qareeb khari hai? Yeh zone clear resistance area hota hai jahan se reversal ya sharp correction ka sab se bara khatra hota hai. 

Aap Infosys partnership aur Standard Chartered ke $200 target ki badi badi batein toh kar rahe hain, lekin kya aapne yeh dekha ke itni badi 1.7 billion bank accounts ki deal ke bawajood short-term mein LINK ki price 4% drop ho gayi? Yeh "sell the news" ka classic trap hota hai jismein retail investors phans jaate hain aur aggressive traders apna nuqsan karwa bethte hain. StockTwits par 70% retail ka bullish hona kisi guarantee ka naam nahi hai; retail sentiment aksar market ke top par hi sab se zyaada over-enthusiastic hota hai.

Mazeed yeh ke global macro environment ko dekhein! Global bond yields aur 5% ki Treasury yields ek tight monetary environment paida kar rahi hain, jo kisi bhi waqt risk-on assets par dabao daal sakti hain. Agar hum aisi jagah par bina soche samjhe naye positions khol lenge jahan volatility aur ATR (0.72) moderate-to-high ho, toh hum firm ki capital ko un zaroori corrections ke samne khol denge jo aksar aisi rallies ke baad aati hain. 

Isi liye, trader ka yeh faisla ke filhal "HOLD" kiya jaye aur kisi minor pullback ka intezar kiya jaye, bilkul aqlmandi ka faisla hai. Darr nahi, isay ehtiyat kehte hain, aur hamara asal maqsad asset ki protection aur steady growth hai, na ke bina wajah ka juwa khelna.
