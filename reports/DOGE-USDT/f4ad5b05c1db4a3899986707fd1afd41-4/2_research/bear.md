### 2. Low Volatility: "Resilience" ya Total Apathy?

Arre bhai, thora aam feham (common sense) istamal karein! Low volatility ka matlab hamesha strength nahi hota; iska matlab "total lack of interest" bhi hota hai. 
Pichle ek mahine (14 September se 14 October 2026) mein `DOGE-USDT` ke liye ek bhi direct positive news ya catalyst samne nahi aaya. Jab market mein koi buyer hi nahi aana chahta aur volume sookh raha ho, toh price girne se bach toh jati hai kyunke sellers bhi thakey hue hain, lekin is stagnant halat ko "bullish base" kehna khud ko andheron mein rakhne ke barabar hai. Isko "Dead Money Syndrome" kehte hain jahan capital block ho jata hai jabke baqi asset classes behtar perform kar rahi hoti hain.
