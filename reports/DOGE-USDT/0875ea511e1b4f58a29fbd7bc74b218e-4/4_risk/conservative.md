Main Aggressive Analyst ki is pur-josh batein sun raha hoon, lekin ek Conservative Risk Analyst ke taur par main yeh kehna chahta hoon ke jazbaat aur "FOMO" (fear of missing out) mein aakar capital risk karna hamari firm ke liye sabse bara khatra hai. Aap yeh keh rahe hain ke yeh tight consolidation range (0.09) ek explosive move ke liye energy jama kar rahi hai, lekin yeh bhi toh sochiye ke agar market niche ki taraf breakout kar gayi aur support toot kar **0.08** (Bollinger Lower Band) par aa gayi, toh hamara kitna bara nuqsaan ho sakta hai? Bina kisi tasdeeq (confirmation) ke andhay dhund position lena gambling hai, trading nahi.

Aapne macro sentiment aur Fed rate hikes ki kam hoti expectations ka hawala diya, lekin sath hi yeh kyun nazar-andaz kar rahe hain ke is saal crypto hacks ki maloomat ke mutabiq $2.5B se zyada hacks ki prediction 98% tak probable hai? Iska matlab yeh hai ke market mein overall security aur systemic risks abhi bhi bohat buland hain. `DOGE-USDT` jesa meme-crypto asset kisi direct fundamental news ke baghair sirf social media sentiment aur liquidity par chalta hai, jo kisi bhi waqt palat sakti hai. RSI ka 55.81 par hona aur MACD ka bilkul flat (0.00) hona is baat ki wazeh daleel hai ke buyers aur sellers dono mein se kisi ka bhi abhi tak koi mazboot control nahi hai. Yeh "wait and watch" karne ka waqt hai, na ke bina wajah ka risk lene ka.

Sideline par baith kar apni capital ko mehfooz rakhna koi kamzori nahi hai, balkay yeh samajhdari hai taake hum apne assets ko unnecessary volatility se bacha sakein. Jab tak price **0.10** resistance ko ya **0.08** support ko tod kar ek saaf directional trend nahi banati, tab tak **HOLD** ki hikmat-e-amli hi hamari firm ki poonji (capital) ke liye sabse mahfooz aur behtareen rasta hai.
