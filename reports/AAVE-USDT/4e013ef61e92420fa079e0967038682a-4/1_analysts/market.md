### **1. Muntakhib Shuda Indicators Aur Unki Wajah (Selected Indicators)**

Market ki maujooda halat (strong bull run, high momentum, aur volatility) ko dekhte hue humne 8 behtareen indicators muntakhib kiye hain jo aapas mein overlap nahi karte aur mukammal view dete hain:

1. **close_10_ema (10-Day Exponential Moving Average):** Short-term momentum aur fast price shifts ko pakadne ke liye, taake current rally ki taqat ka andaza ho sakay.
2. **close_50_sma (50-Day Simple Moving Average):** Medium-term trend direction aur dynamic support levels ko track karne ke liye.
3. **close_200_sma (200-Day Simple Moving Average):** Long-term trend benchmark aur overall market structure ki taqat ko confirm karne ke liye.
4. **rsi (Relative Strength Index):** Momentum ko napne ke liye taake pata chal sakay ke asset overbought zone ke qareeb hai ya nahi, aur reversal ya continuation ka andaza ho.
5. **macd (Moving Average Convergence Divergence):** Trend ke changes aur momentum ki strength ko compute karne ke liye.
6. **boll_ub (Bollinger Upper Band):** Breakout zones aur overbought conditions ko pehchanne ke liye jab price volatility expand ho rahi ho.
7. **boll_lb (Bollinger Lower Band):** Potential support aur lower volatility boundaries ke liye.
8. **atr (Average True Range):** Volatility measure karne ke liye taake stop-loss levels aur position sizing theek se manage ki ja sakay.
