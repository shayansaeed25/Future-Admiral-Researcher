### 1. Macro-Economic Reality: Fed Rate Cuts ka Khatam Hona
Aapne macro sentiment ka zikr kiya, lekin aap yeh bhool gaye ke prediction markets aur Polymarket par **96% probability** is baat ki hai ke **2026 mein koi Fed rate cut nahi hoga!** Yeh koi mamooli baat nahi hai, bhai. Iska matlab hai ke global liquidity tight rehne wali hai. Moody’s ke Mark Zandi tak ne khabardad kar diya hai ke unche interest rates pehle hi real economy ko andar se khokhla kar rahe hain. 
Aap risk-on sentiment aur tech stocks ki choti si rally ko dekh kar khush ho rahe hain, lekin jabasli liquidity market se gayab ho aur paisa mehnga ho, toh crypto jaise high-beta assets sab se pehle crash karte hain. Yeh "rocket fuel" nahi, balkay sookhi lakdi hai jo zara si aag se rakh ho sakti hai!

### 2. Momentum ka Thanda Parna aur MACD Histogram
Aap keh rahe hain ke MACD ka negative histogram (-138.70) aur RSI (62.04) sirf aik "healthy pause" hai aur price seedha Bollinger Upper Band ($89,015.97) ki taraf jayegi. Bhai, yeh aapki over-optimistic assumption hai! 
Jab momentum cool-off hota hai aur histogram negative mein jata hai, toh yeh is baat ki nishandahi karta hai ke buyers ki taqat khatam ho rahi hai aur sellers market par qabza karne ki koshish kar rahe hain. Agar price ne **10 EMA ($84,168.48)** ya **Bollinger Middle ($82,766.35)** ka level tora, toh yeh "healthy pause" foran aik deep correction mein badal jayegi, aur aapki saari accumulation dhar ki dhar reh jayegi.

Bhai, maan liya ke price apne 50 SMA ($79,007) aur 200 SMA ($71,509) se oopar hai, lekin zara sochein ke gap kitna bara hai! Aapke paas sirf aik hi rasta hai: blind optimism.
