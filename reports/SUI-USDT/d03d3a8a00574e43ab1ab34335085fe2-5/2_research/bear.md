Jab yeh bubble phatega aur price wapas 1.07 ya 0.88 ki taraf slide karegi, tab aapka yeh "macro control" kahan jaye ga? - Jab momentum thakta hai aur log yeh samajhte hain ke "abhi aur oopar jayega," tabhi "smart money" apna profit book karke nikal jati hai aur retail investors (aapse aur hum jaise log) oopri satah (tops) par phans jate hain. MACD line ka signal line ke thora sa oopar hona sirf aik dying gasp hai, strong trend nahi.
