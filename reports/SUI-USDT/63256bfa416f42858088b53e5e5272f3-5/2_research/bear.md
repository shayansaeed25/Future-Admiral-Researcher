### 2. Overextended Moving Averages aur Mean Reversion Ka Khatra
Aapne **close_200_sma (0.85)** aur **close_50_sma (0.88)** ka hawala de kar kaha ke price (1.20) inase kaafi ooper hai. Statistics aur market mechanics ke mutabiq, jab price apne moving averages se itna ziyada dur nikal jati hai, toh **Mean Reversion** ka kanoon harkat mein aata hai. Iska matlab hai ke price ko wapas apni average (yani 0.85 - 0.88 ki zone) ki taraf aana hi padta hai. Jo log 1.20 par gari mein chadh rahe hain, woh us waqt buri tarah phansenge jab correction aayegi.

Zara sa bhi selling pressure aate hi RSI foran overbought se drop karega aur panic selling shuru ho jayegi.

Aapka yeh kehna ke "bear ki koi tuk nahi banti," bilkul kham khayali hai kyunki market mein sabse bada risk wahi hota hai jab aap over-confidence ka shikar ho jayein.
