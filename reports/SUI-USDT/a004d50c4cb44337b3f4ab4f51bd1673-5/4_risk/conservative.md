Yeh "saansein durust karne" ka waqt nahi hai, yeh market ka woh phase hai jahan sharp correction ya pullback ka sabse bara khatra hota hai.

Yeh sirf broader market hype par chal raha hai, aur jab market bina kisi fundamental news ke sirf hype par upar jati hai, toh utni hi tezi se neeche bhi girti hai.
