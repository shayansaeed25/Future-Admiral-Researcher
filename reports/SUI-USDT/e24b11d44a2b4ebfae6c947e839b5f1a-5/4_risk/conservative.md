Aap yeh bhool rahe hain ke hal hi mein price 6 October ko $1.13 se gir kar 7 October ko achanak $1.03 tak aa gayi thi. Yeh choti moti utaar charhao (swings) is baat ki nishandahi karti hain ke market mein instability mojood hai. Maan liya ke price 50 SMA ($0.91) aur 200 SMA ($0.86) se oopar hai aur long-term trend bullish hai, lekin iska hargiz yeh matlab nahi hai ke hum short-term khatrat ko nazar andaz kar dein. MACD histogram ka negative (-0.02) hona is baat ka wazeh saboot hai ke bullish momentum dheema par raha hai aur market mein selling pressure ya consolidation ka phase chal raha hai. Isay "healthy pause" keh kar nazar andaz karna ek bari kham-khayali hai.

Mazeed yeh ke, ATR $0.08 par hone ka matlab hai ke volatility active hai. Agar hum bina kisi mazboot confirmation ya clear breakout ke aggressive entry le lein aur market mazeed correction ki taraf chali gayi, toh hamara set kiya gaya stop-loss (0.96) hit ho sakta hai, jis se firm ko bila-waja ka nuqsan bardasht karna padega. 

Global macro environment bhi filhal mixed chal raha hai, aur tech ya high-beta crypto assets mein kab sentiment badal jaye kuch kaha nahi ja sakta. Isliye, jald bazi mein exposure barhana ya risk lena aqalmandi nahi hai. Hamari pehli tarjeeh sarmaye ki hifazat (asset protection) aur volatility ko minimize karna honi chahiye. Isi liye "Hold" karna aur market ke mazeed saaf hone ka intezar karna hi is waqt sabse mehfooz aur behtareen rasta hai.
