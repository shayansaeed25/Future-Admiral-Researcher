2. **Bollinger Lower Band aur Support Test ka Asli Khatra:** 
Aap keh rahe hain ke Bollinger Lower Band (109.25) par price ka hona "golden entry point" hai. Bhai, zara hosh ke naakhun lein! Yeh lower band ko test karna har waqt bounce nahi deta; kai baar yeh breakout ya breakdown ki shuruat hoti hai. RSI 45.31 par neutral zone mein zaroor hai, lekin yeh downward slope par hai. Iska matlab hai ke market mein abhi mazeed girawat ki gunjaish baaqi hai. Bina kisi confirmation ke entry lena matlab apne paon par khud kulhadi marna hai.

3. **Polymarket Sentiment aur Macro Risks ko Nazar-Andaz Karna:**

Crypto jaisi volatile market mein in cheezon ko ignore karna sabse badi bewakoofi hai.
