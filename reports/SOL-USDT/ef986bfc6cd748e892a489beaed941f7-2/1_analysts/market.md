Market ki mojooda halat aur volatility ko dekhte hue, hum ne niche diye gaye up to 8 indicators select kiye hain jo aik doosrey ke mutabiq hain aur baghair kisi redundancy ke mukammal tasveer pesh karte hain:

7. **atr (Average True Range)**: Stop-loss levels set karne aur risk management ke liye.
