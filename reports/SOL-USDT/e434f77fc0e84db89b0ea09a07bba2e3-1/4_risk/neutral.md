Hamein aik middle ground yaani "Scaled-in Approach" ya "Conditional Strategy" apnani chahiye. Trader ka current "Hold" ka faisla is waqt ke liye theek hai kyunki hum mazeed direction clear hone ka wait kar rahe hain, lekin iska matlab yeh nahi ke hum aglay kuch dino ke liye blind ho jayein.

Is tarah hum aggressive growth ke mauqe ko bhi haath se nahi jane denge aur conservative safety margins (jaise ATR ke mutabiq tight stop-loss) ke zariye apne capital ko bhi bacha lenge. Dono extremes ko chhoriye aur smart, balanced risk management ki taraf aaiye!
