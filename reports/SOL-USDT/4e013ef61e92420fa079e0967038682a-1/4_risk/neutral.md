Dusri taraf, Conservative Analyst bhai, aapki ahtiyat apni jagah theek hai, lekin aapka yeh kehna ke "Maintain existing exposure" ke ilawa kuch sochna hi nahi chahiye, market ke bullish trend aur macro tailwinds ko bilkul ignore karta hai. Sirf is darr se ke market mein volatility hai, hum poori tarah hath par hath dhar kar nahi baith sakte. Agar hum bilkul hi passive ho jayenge, toh hum market ke asli upside potential se mahroom reh jayenge.

Toh phir hamara raasta kya hona chahiye? Na toh Aggressive bhai ki tarah andha risk lein aur na hi Conservative bhai ki tarah bilkul dar kar baith jayen. Ek balanced aur moderate strategy yeh kehti hai ke hum filhal apna existing exposure maintain rakhein, lekin sath hi apne alert levels set karein. Agar price $129.86 ki resistance ko volume ke sath break karti hai, ya phir thori correction ke baad support ($114–$118 range) par aakar bounce leti hai, tab hum apne ATR ($5.00) ko zehan mein rakhte huay calculated aur safe entries le sakte hain. Yeh approach dono taraf ke khatrat se bachate huay behtareen aur sustainable nateeja degi!
