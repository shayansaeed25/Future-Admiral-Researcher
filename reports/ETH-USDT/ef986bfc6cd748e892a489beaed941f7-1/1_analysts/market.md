### 1. Muntakhib Shuda Indicator Category aur Unka Tawazun (Indicator Selection)
Market ki mojooda halat (volatile correction aur trend continuation) ko samajhnay ke liye hum ne 8 ahem indicators muntakhib kiye hain jo kisi bhi kisam ki redundancy ke baghair complementary maloomat faraham karte hain:
1. **close_200_sma (200 SMA):** Long-term trend benchmark jo market ke buniyadi rujhan (bullish structure) ki tasdeeq karta hai.
2. **close_50_sma (50 SMA):** Medium-term trend indicator jo dynamic support aur trend direction ki nishandahi karta hai.
3. **close_10_ema (10 EMA):** Responsive short-term average jo price ki taizi ya mandi ke fori shifts ko pakarta hai.
4. **macd (MACD):** Momentum aur EMA differences ke zariye trend changes aur momentum ki taqat ko measure karta hai.
5. **rsi (RSI):** Relative Strength Index jo overbought/oversold halat ko flag karta hai.
6. **boll (Bollinger Middle):** 20 SMA par mabni dynamic benchmark jo bands ke sath mil kar volatility aur breakout zones dikhata hai.
7. **atr (ATR):** Average True Range jo market volatility ki pemaish karta hai taake risk management aur stop-loss theek se set kiye ja saken.
8. **vwma (VWMA):** Volume Weighted Moving Average jo price action ko volume ke sath integrate karke trend ki authenticity verify karta hai.
