### **Mantiq-e-Intikhab-e-Indicators (Indicator Selection Rationale)**

Market ke mojooda halaat aur volatility ko dekhte huay, hum ne chand behtareen aur gair-mutanaqiz (non-redundant) indicators ka intekhab kiya hai jo ke mukhtalif categories se taaluq rakhtay hain taake aik mukamal aur saaf tasveer saamne aa sakay:

1. **close_200_sma (Moving Average):** Yeh long-term trend benchmark hai jo long-term market ki direction confirm karta hai.
2. **close_50_sma (Moving Average):** Medium-term trend aur dynamic support/resistance levels ko pehchannay ke liye behtareen hai.
3. **close_10_ema (Moving Average):** Short-term momentum aur quick shifts ko pakarney ke liye istemal hota hai.
4. **rsi (Momentum Indicator):** Overbought aur oversold conditions ko naapne ke liye aik hi momentum indicator kafi hai (taake redundancy se bacha ja sakay).
5. **boll / boll_ub / boll_lb (Volatility Indicators):** Bollinger Bands price ki volatility aur potential breakout/reversal zones ko samajhne ke liye ek complete package hain.
6. **macd (MACD Related):** Trend changes aur momentum crossovers ka andaza lagane ke liye.
