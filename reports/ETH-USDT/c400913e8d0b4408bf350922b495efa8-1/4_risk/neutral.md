Is liye, na toh Aggressive Analyst ki andhi tezi theek hai aur na hi Conservative Analyst ka khauf ka maahol. Behtareen raasta yeh hai ke hum ek moderate aur balanced strategy apnayein. Hum filhal foran poori capital ke sath jump nahi karenge, lekin is market drop ko ek watch-list opportunity banayenge. Jab tak price Bollinger Lower Band ($2,561.32) ke andar wapas aakar stabilize nahi hoti aur MACD histogram thora negative se neutral nahi hota, tab tak hum wait karenge. Is tarah hum aggressive risk aur conservative paralysis dono se bach kar ek smart aur sustainable trading approach ikhtiyar kar sakte hain.
