Mera neutral aur balanced risk analysis yeh kehta hai ke humein na toh aggressive analyst ki tarah andha-dhoond naye exposure barhane ki zaroorat hai, aur na hi conservative analyst ki tarah itna darpook banna chahiye ke hum market ke har consolidation phase ko aik disaster samajh lein. 

Humein ek moderate aur disciplined rasta apnana chahiye. Is waqt sab se behtareen hikmat-e-amli yeh hai ke hum apne trader ke "HOLD" ke faislay par qaim rahein, lekin sath hi apni risk management ko mazeed behter karein.
