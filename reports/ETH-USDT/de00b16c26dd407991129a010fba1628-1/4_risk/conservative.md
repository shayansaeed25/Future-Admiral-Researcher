Agar hum is waqt bina kisi clear breakout ke naye positions le lenge, toh hum khud ko unnecessary downside risk mein daal rahe honge.

Yeh achi baat hai, lekin kya aapne Goldman Sachs ki warning ko bhula diya? American debt pile aur rising interest rates par jo sawal uthaye gaye hain, woh kisi bhi waqt bond market aur broader risk assets mein sudden volatility paida kar sakte hain. Crypto market in macroeconomic shocks se alag nahi hai. Bollinger Bands is waqt yeh bata rahe hain ke qeemat **2605.82** (Lower Band) aur **2758.24** (Upper Band) ke beech mein phansi hui hai aur low volatility ke sath consolidate kar rahi hai. Jab tak Upper Band volume ke sath break nahi hota, tab tak naye positions kholna qamar par khud hi kulhadi marne ke barabar hai.

Mera yeh saaf kehna hai ke "Hold" karna is waqt sabse mehfooz aur aaqilana hikmat-e-amli hai. Hamara maqsad raaton-raat ameer hona nahi, balkay firm ke assets ko mehfooz rakhna aur steady growth ensure karna hai.
