Lekin unhein yeh yaad rakhna chahiye ke yeh negative momentum is baat ki wazeh nishandahi karta hai ke short-term mein buyers ka zor kam ho raha hai aur sellers ya profit-takers active ho sakte hain. Jab momentum negative ho, toh bina kisi taqatwar confirmation ke naya risk lena kisi disaster se kam nahi.

Yeh theek hai ke global equities aur tech stocks record highs par hain, lekin crypto market ka apna aik volatile mizaj hota hai. ATR (Average True Range) 78.65 par moderate volatility dikha raha hai, aur agar yeh consolidation downside par breakdown kar gayi, toh woh 74% probability palak jhapakte hi khatam ho sakti hai. Macro optimism hamesha short-term crypto corrections ko nahi roak sakta.

Jab tak price $2,713.75 par aik clear breakout nahi deti, tab tak aggressive accumulation karna sirf "opportunity cost" bachane ke chakkar mein apna poora capital daav par lagana hai.

Isi liye, mera yeh sakht moqqaf hai ke **HOLD** ka faisla hi is waqt sab se mehfooz aur aqlmandi ka qadam hai. Hamara maqsad raaton-raat ameer hona nahi, balki apnay assets ko mehfooz rakhte huwe aage barhna hai. Jab tak market clear direction ya positive crossover nahi deti, hum kisi bhi gair-zaroori risk ka shikaar nahi honge.
