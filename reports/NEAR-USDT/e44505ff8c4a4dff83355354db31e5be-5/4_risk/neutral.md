
Neutral Analyst: Salam dosto! Main Neutral Risk Analyst hoon, aur maine dono taraf ki baatein bohot ghaur se suni hain. Ab waqt aa gaya hai ke dono analysts ke nazariyon ka thora critical jaiza liya jaye aur dekha jaye ke haqeeqat kahan lie karti hai.

Sab se pehle, Aggressive Analyst bhai ki baat karte hain. Aapka yeh kehna bilkul theek hai ke NEAR-USDT ka momentum bohot strong hai, aur Bitwise Spot ETF ya Ondo Finance ke sath partnership jese fundamentals is asset ko ek lambi race ka ghoda banatay hain. 60% StockTwits sentiment aur zero bearish posts yeh batatay hain ke retail mein abhi bhi Josh hai. Lekin bhai, aap yahan ek ahem khatray ko nazar-andaaz kar rahay hain. Jab RSI 79.92 par ho aur price $1.80 se chal kar seedha $5.21 tak pohnch jaye, toh bina kisi pullback ya consolidation ke naye positions kholna gambling ke barabar ho jata hai. BeInCrypto ki report ka yeh point bilkul durust hai ke ETP ki growth ziyadatar price rally ki wajah se hai, na ke bilkul naye organic capital ki wajah se. Sirf hype par andhadhundh paisa lagana aggressive nahi, balkay reckless hai.

Dusri taraf, Conservative Analyst bhai, aapka darr apni jagah theek hai ke rising bond yields (5% tak) aur overextended rally ek correction ko invite kar sakti hai. Global macro pressure real hai. Lekin aapka yeh kehna ke "sab doob jayega" aur har naye mauqay ko bilkul reject kar dena, ek aisi conservative soch hai jo humein hamesha peeche rakhti hai. Agar hum sirf darr ki wajah se baith rahein ge, toh jab altcoins Bitcoin aur Ethereum ko outpace kar rahe hon ge, hum saara bada profit miss kar dein ge. Trader ne jo $4.76 par stop-loss lagaya hai, woh sirf ek number nahi hai, balkay ek achi safety net hai jo aapke dar ko kaafi had tak khatam kar deti hai.

Toh phir behtareen raasta kya hai? Na toh Aggressive analyst ki tarah andha risk lein aur na hi Conservative analyst ki tarah darr kar saaray mauqay ganwa dein. Hamari neutral aur moderate strategy yeh honi chahiye ke hum trader ke "Hold" ke faislay par qaim rahein aur maujuda holdings ko bilkul na chheren. Naye positions kholne ke liye hum us waqt tak intezaar karein jab tak market thori consolidate ho kar kisi healthy support level par na aaye. Is tarah hum momentum ka faida bhi utha sakte hain aur overbought zone ke short-term correction ya macro volatility se apne aap ko mehfooz bhi rakh sakte hain. Yeh hai woh balanced approach jo risk aur reward dono ko barabar rakhti hai!
